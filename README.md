# Monte-Carlo-Option-Pricing-in-VBA-GBM-Exotic-Options-Structured-Products
Monte Carlo pricing in Excel/VBA: GBM path simulation, vanilla and exotic options (Asian, lookback) and a Worst-Of certificate on three correlated assets. (MANU, JUVE, ENEL)
## Overview

This project implements Monte Carlo option pricing entirely in Excel with VBA, progressing from basic path simulation to exotic options and a multi-asset structured product. It is Report 6 of the A.Y. 2025-2026 coursework. All simulations use the exact log-normal discretisation of Geometric Brownian Motion under the risk-neutral measure, with Gaussian draws generated by the Box-Muller transform applied to VBA's `Rnd()`.

### GBM paths

100 trajectories are simulated and plotted in an embedded Excel chart, showing how the log-normal fan widens with σ√T while the median follows the risk-free drift.

### Vanilla pricers

European calls and puts are priced with three approaches: a one-step terminal-price sampler with antithetic variates, a multi-step scheme, and a closed-form GBM step. Each is compared against the Black-Scholes benchmark (510 simulations, 504 steps). The residual differences are consistent with Monte Carlo noise.

| Method | MC Call (€) | BS Call (€) | Diff | MC Put (€) | BS Put (€) | Diff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-step closed form | 172.16 | 162.33 | 9.83 | 71.34 | 65.35 | 5.99 |
| Multi-step | 164.77 | 162.33 | 2.44 | 66.51 | 65.35 | 1.16 |
| Closed GBM step | 151.53 | 162.33 | -10.79 | 60.18 | 65.35 | -5.17 |

### Exotic options

Arithmetic Asian options are cheaper than vanillas because averaging lowers effective volatility (≈ σ/√3). Floating-strike lookback calls and puts track the running minimum and maximum of each path and are compared with the Goldman-Sosin-Gatto closed form. Discrete monitoring introduces a known bias that shrinks as the number of steps grows.

| Asian option | MC Call (€) | MC Put (€) | St dev Call (€) | St dev Put (€) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic average | 83.39 | 37.32 | 3.05 | 2.08 |

| Lookback option | MC Call (€) | Benchmark Call (€) | MC Put (€) | Benchmark Put (€) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-step | 243.06 | 187.71 | 184.19 | 130.61 |
| Closed GBM step | 234.47 | 187.71 | 178.58 | 130.61 |

### Worst-Of certificate

A 3-year Maxi Cash Collect on Juventus, Manchester United and Enel is priced using volatilities and correlations estimated from two years of Refinitiv daily data, with correlated paths generated through a Cholesky decomposition.

| Asset | Spot S₀ (€) | Volatility σ | ρ with JUVE | ρ with MANU | ρ with ENEL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Juventus (JUVE) | 2.07 | 43.15% | 1.000 | 0.040 | 0.106 |
| Manchester United (MANU) | 17.54 | 32.59% | 0.040 | 1.000 | 0.041 |
| Enel (ENEL) | 9.78 | 19.22% | 0.106 | 0.041 | 1.000 |

| Certificate term | Value |
|---|---|
| Notional | €100 per certificate |
| Maturity | 3 years (Nov 2025 – Nov 2028) |
| Initial coupon | 22% unconditional |
| Quarterly coupon | 1% (4% p.a.), conditional, with memory effect |
| Coupon barrier | 50% of initial level |
| Capital barrier | 60% of initial level |
| Autocall trigger | 100% (from August 2026) |

| Output | Value |
|---|---|
| MC fair value | €79.80 |
| Standard deviation | €1.13 |
| Price / notional | 79.80% |

The fair value is below the 100% issue price, which implies an issuer margin at inception.

**Tech:** stochastic simulation, variance reduction, path-dependent derivatives, Cholesky-based correlation modelling, structured-product valuation, VBA.
